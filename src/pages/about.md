# About DIMO

DIMO is the vehicle data infrastructure that powers the session-based economy.
We give developers a single, permissioned API to real-time data from vehicles
across the 50+ brands already connected to DIMO, so teams can build rentals,
pay-per-use, fleet, insurance, and AI applications without integrating each make
themselves.

## What we build

- **Works with any make:** one API to vehicle data across the 50+ brands already
  connected to DIMO, with normalized real-time telemetry, vehicle identity, and
  event webhooks. DIMO runs direct integrations like the
  [Tesla oracle](https://github.com/DIMO-Network/tesla-oracle), and you can
  connect any other source by building on the
  [oracle example](https://github.com/DIMO-Network/oracle-example). Want a
  direct integration for your brand? [Reach out to us](/contact).
- **Permissioned by design:** vehicle owners grant and revoke access through the
  SACD consent model. Developers only ever see what a user has explicitly
  shared. This is built to meet GDPR and the EU Data Act.
- **AI-ready:** Agent skills and MCP-accessible data streams for building AI
  applications over real vehicle data.
- **Open core:** the protocol and core tooling are open source, so there is no
  vendor lock-in.

## Our story

DIMO was founded in 2021 around a frustration familiar to anyone who has shipped
a connected-vehicle product: the data exists, but reaching it means a separate
contract, a separate API, and a separate consent story for every manufacturer.
Most teams spend their first quarter building integration plumbing instead of
their product.

So we built the plumbing once, in the open. Drivers connect their own cars to
the network (more than 50 brands so far), and developers get one permissioned
API for telemetry, identity, and consent. The protocol and core tooling are open
source, which means anyone can audit how access control actually works rather
than taking our word for it.

Today DIMO powers rentals, fleet tools, insurance products, EV charging apps,
and AI agents that act on real vehicle data with the owner's explicit, revocable
permission.

## Our mission

We believe the people who drive vehicles should control the data those vehicles
produce, and that open infrastructure, not walled gardens, is how the next
generation of mobility products gets built. DIMO was founded in 2021 to make
that infrastructure real.
