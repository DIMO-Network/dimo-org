Last Updated: June 5, 2023

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Infrastructure Inc, a Delaware C
Corporation based in the USA ("DINC," "we," "our," or "us"), might collect,
store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our
services ("Services"), such as when you:

- Visit our website at dinc.dev, or any website of ours that links to this
  privacy notice
- Download and use our mobile application (DIMO Mobile), or any other
  application of ours that links to this privacy notice
- Engage with us in other related ways, including any sales, marketing, or
  events

Questions or concerns? Reading this privacy policy will help you understand your
privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices,
please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns,
please contact us at privacy@dinc.dev.

We develop software on open connectivity platforms for IoT devices, primarily
vehicles. We are committed to privacy and this Privacy Policy applies when you
use our platform, marketplace, website and other online products or devices
(collectively, our "Platform"), contact our customer service team, engage with
us on social media, or otherwise interact with us.

We may change this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will
notify you by revising the date at the top of this policy and, when material, we
will provide you with additional notice (such as adding a statement to our
website or sending you a notification) and obtain consent as required under
applicable law. Where permissible under applicable privacy laws, your continued
use of this Platform after we make changes is deemed to be acceptance of those
changes. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy regularly to stay
informed about our information practices and the choices available to you.

## COLLECTION OF INFORMATION

### Information You Provide to Us

To use the Platform, you must enroll and agree to log-in to certain features
through designated third-party provider accounts. If you log-in through such
third party accounts, such third party will share certain of your personal data
with us in accordance with the terms you have agreed with such third party. In
other cases, you may provide personal information directly to us. For example,
you share information directly with us when you complete a digital form, submit
or post content through our Platform, make a purchase, communicate with us via
third-party platforms, request customer support, or otherwise communicate with
us. The types of personal information we may collect include your name, email
address, vehicle identification number (VIN), digital asset wallet address and
any other information you choose to provide.

The Platform is not directed to children under the age of 16. If you are a
parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with
personal information without your consent, please contact us at
privacy@dinc.dev. If we become aware that a child under the age of 16 has
provided us with personal information, we would contact the user, verify their
age and, if necessary, seek the consent of the parent/guardian before deleting
their data.

You can change your sharing preferences at any time under Settings to what
information you wish to share. By default, all sharing of personal information
will be turned off.

Personal & Sensitive Information Provided by You. The personal information that
we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the Services,
the choices you make, and the products and features you use. Depending on your
choices in the Settings, the personal information we collect may include the
following:

- Vehicle metadata (e.g., VIN, Make, Model, Year)
- Telemetry device attributes (e.g., serial number)
- Vehicle location information
- Vehicle telemetry data including
  - steering - angle of wheels/steering wheel
  - pedals - throttle (% applied)
  - pedals - brake (% applied)
  - wheels - speed per tire (lf/lr/rl/rr)
  - speed - car speed (on/off)
  - metadata - odometer (odometer from vehicle)
  - wheels - pressure per tire (lf/lr/rl/rr)
  - apertures - door status per door (driver, passenger, trunk)
  - apertures - window status per window (open/closed)
  - lights - turn signals status (left/right on/off)
  - lights - running lights (on/off)
  - recovery - low beam (on/off)
  - recovery - high beam (on/off)
  - seat belts - buckled/unbuckled per seat (lf/lr/rl/rr)
  - battery - state of health (as reported)
  - battery - battery voltage (overall battery voltage)
  - battery - battery capacity remaining (in kwh)
  - battery - battery temperature
  - battery - battery resistance
  - battery - battery information per cell (volts/amps) (values per battery
    cell)
  - battery - estimated range (as reported by the car)
  - charging - charging (yes/no)
  - charging - charging rate (kwh)
  - charging - charging voltage (in volts)
  - charging - charging amperage (in amps)
  - charging - charging time to full
  - regenerative braking - recovery rate (kwh)
  - ADAS - engaged yes/no
  - ADAS - disengagements (information about disengagements)
- Document photos and associated metadata when you upload license, title,
  registration, lease, and/or insurance documents
- User preferences and identity including email, wallet address, name, region,
  app preferences, and app usage which may reveal race or ethnic origin

If you wish to learn more about the above the personal information and sensitive
information fields, please read the details under Settings before opting in to
sharing your data.

### Information We Collect Automatically When You Interact with Us

When you access or use our Platform or otherwise transact business with us, we
automatically collect the following information:

- Log and Usage Data: Log and usage data is service-related, diagnostic, usage,
  and performance information our servers automatically collect when you access
  or use our Services and which we record in log files. Depending on how you
  interact with us, this log data may include your IP address, device
  information, browser type, and settings and information about your activity in
  the Services (such as the date/time stamps associated with your usage, pages
  and files viewed, searches, and other actions you take such as which features
  you use), device event information (such as system activity, error reports
  (sometimes called "crash dumps"), and hardware settings).
- Transactional Information: When you make a purchase, we (and our service
  providers) collect information about the transaction, such as product details,
  purchase price, and the date and location of the transaction.
- Device and Usage Information: We collect information about how you access our
  Platform, including data about the device and network you use, such as your
  hardware model, operating system version, mobile network, IP address, unique
  device identifiers, browser type, and app version. We also collect information
  about your activity on our Platform, such as access times, pages viewed, links
  clicked, and the page you visited before navigating to our Platform.
- Information Collected by Cookies and Similar Tracking Technologies: We (and
  our service providers) use tracking technologies, such as cookies and web
  beacons, to collect information about you. Cookies are small data files stored
  on your hard drive or in device memory that help us improve our Platform and
  your experience, see which areas and features of our Platform are popular, and
  count visits. Web beacons (also known as "pixel tags" or "clear GIFs") are
  electronic images that we use on our Platform and in our emails to help
  deliver cookies, count visits, and understand usage and campaign
  effectiveness. For more information about cookies and how to disable them, see
  our [Cookie Policy](/docs/dinc/cookies-policy) and the Your Choices section
  below.

### Information We Collect from Other Sources

We obtain information from third-parties to email you when necessary and obtain
analytics of you as a user, to the extent permitted by your local rules and
regulations. Additionally, if you create or log into DINC sites or DIMO mobile
through a third-party platform (such as Google), we will collect certain
information from that platform, such as your name, birthday, and profile
picture, in accordance with the authorization procedures determined by such

### Information We Derive

We may derive information or draw inferences about you based on the information

your IP address, VIN, or wallet address.

## TRANSFER OF INFORMATION TO THE UNITED STATES AND OTHER COUNTRIES

DINC is headquartered in the United States, and we have operations and service
providers in the United States and other countries. Therefore, we and our
service providers will transfer your personal information to, or store or access
it in, jurisdictions that may not provide levels of data protection that are
equivalent to those of your home jurisdiction. However, to ensure that your
personal information receives an adequate level of protection in the
jurisdictions in which we process it we encrypt all at-rest data and on-transit
data with the TLS 1.2 protocol.

European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses:

We have implemented measures to protect your personal information, including by
using the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers of
personal information between our group companies and between us and our approved
third-party providers mentioned above. These clauses require all recipients to
protect all personal information that they process originating from the EEA or
UK in accordance with European data protection laws and regulations. Our
Standard Contractual Clauses can be provided upon request.

## ADDITIONAL DISCLOSURES FOR INDIVIDUALS IN EUROPE

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or
Switzerland, you have certain rights and protections under the law regarding the
processing of your personal data, and this section applies to you.

### Legal Basis for Processing

When DINC processes your personal data, DINC will do so in reliance on the
following lawful bases:

- To perform our responsibilities under our contract with you (e.g., processing
  payments for and providing the products and Platform you requested). [Art. 6
  Par. 1 lit. b GDPR]
- When we have a legitimate interest in processing your personal data to operate
  our business or protect our interests (e.g., to provide, maintain, and improve
  our products and Platform, conduct data analytics, and communicate with you).
  [Art. 6 Par. 1 lit. f GDPR]
- To comply with our legal obligations (e.g., to maintain a record of your
  consents and track those who have opted out of marketing communications).
  [Art. 6 Par. 1 lit. c GDPR]
- When we have your consent to do so (e.g., when you enroll and opt in to
  receive marketing communications from us and our partners and/or enroll in the
  token rewards program). When consent is the legal basis for our processing of
  your personal data, you may withdraw such consent at any time (free of charge)
  and with effect for the future. [Art. 6 Par. 1 lit. a GDPR]

### Data Retention

We store personal data associated with your enrollment for as long as your use
of our services and products remains active. In order to close your account,
please contact us at privacy@dinc.dev. If you close your account and terminate
your affiliation with DIMO, we will deactivate your account and delete any data
that may be traceable back to you within 30 days; otherwise, we will delete your
account data after thirty-six (36) months of inactivity. We will anonymize (make
it untraceable back to you) and store other personal data for as long as
necessary to carry out the purposes for which we originally collected it and for
other legitimate business purposes, including to meet our legal, regulatory, or
other compliance obligations.

### Data Subject Rights and Requests [Art. 15 - 21 GDPR]

Subject to certain limitations, you have the right to request access to the
personal data we hold about you and to receive your data in a portable format
and/or make it available for a third party named by you, the right to ask that
your personal data be corrected, completed or erased, and the right to object
to, or request that we restrict, certain processing. If you would like to
exercise any of these rights, please email us at privacy@dinc.dev. If you revoke
your consent or object to processing, we will no longer process your data and
delete it, unless the processing is permitted on another legal basis.
